Water Level Analytics

Detailed borehole reservoir capacity and physical thresholds

Borewell Depth Calibration & Equations

The borewell has an engineered sensor casing with total depth \(H_{total} = 200\text{ cm}\). The ultrasonic sensor is stationed securely at the wellhead, projecting ultrasonic sound pulses downwards at the water surface.

NORMAL STATE (≥ 30 cm)

Water level is adequate for regular irrigation/household use. Green LED is energized, Submersible Pump is OFF, and Buzzer is silent.

LOW STATE (15 cm to 29.9 cm)

Water level is low. Relay is triggered to turn the Pump ON to recharge or transfer water. Buzzer remains OFF to prevent disturbance.

CRITICAL STATE (< 15 cm)

Water level is dangerously low. Pump is shut OFF immediately to prevent dry-running motor failure. Red LED blinks and Buzzer is activated.

Alerts & Safety Management

Audible and visual threshold warnings for borehole water levels

LOW WATER ALERT THRESHOLD 15 cm - 29.9 cm
Warning: Borewell water level is low.
System action: Pump is switched ON to refill. Buzzer stays OFF.
CRITICAL DRY-RUN ALERT < 15 cm
Critical Alert: Borewell water level is critically low.
System action: Pump is instantly turned OFF to prevent motor burnout. Buzzer sounds active audio alert.

About Project

Smart Borewell Water Level Monitoring System Specification

Project Abstract

This Smart Borewell Water Level Monitoring System uses an ESP32 and ultrasonic sensor to measure the distance between the sensor and the water surface. The system calculates the water level and percentage and provides visual and audible alerts when the water level becomes low or critical.
